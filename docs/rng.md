Random numbers are generated by a 128-bit Xorshift with shifts (L15, R4, R21).

Its period is about 2^128, roughly 36 *jō* (3.6 × 10^29) years.

It may help to picture it as one enormous ring.

---

The initial RNG state is set to a fixed value "at the moment you select the game title on the Home screen".

This value is the same for every player and every save file.

Save data, equipment, 3DS vs Switch, the system clock, MHXX vs MHGU: none of these matter.

In other words, if the RNG advances by the same amount after selecting the game on the Home screen, every player gets the same charm.

---

When a charm is obtained from a quest, it consumes 4 or 7 RNG values (without or with a second skill). When a charm is obtained from melding, it consumes 4 or 6.

This is because melding does not roll whether the second skill is positive or negative.

As a result, the patterns of charms you can get from melding and from quests differ.

---

The RNG values that decide a charm are fixed at the moment you select "Yes" to "Spend ◯◯ Wycademy Points. Is that OK?" at the Melding Pot, or at the moment you press "Appraise" in the quest rewards.

---

The RNG basically advances by 1 per frame.

It can also be consumed by various other things.

On the title screen, the RNG only advances with time, except in the following case.

Pressing "continue" on the title screen advances it by about an extra 700±30. This happens each time you press it.

(So if you wait 1 million frames on the title screen, the RNG advances by exactly 1 million.)

Loading a save file advances it by an extra amount. This happens each time you go back to the title screen and load.

In the village (Kokoto), standing at the starting position, it advances by about 1980–1990 every 1800 frames.

It probably also advances by extra amounts during quests.
